# Physics Mechanics Study Guide

A structured mechanics reference for CS and engineering students covering measurements, motion, dynamics, energy, momentum, oscillations, and waves.

## How to Use This Guide
- Start with measurements and vectors if you need notation and unit review.
- Use the motion and Newton's laws sections for force/kinematics problems.
- Jump to the spring and pendulum chapters for SHM-focused exam preparation.
- Keep **11-Key-Equations.md** open as a formula sheet while solving exercises.
- Finish with **12-Problem-Solving.md** for decision trees and common error checks.

## Mechanics Map
| Topic | Core Idea | Most Useful When |
|---|---|---|
| Measurements | Correct units and vectors | Checking setup before solving |
| 1D / 2D Motion | Position, velocity, acceleration | Kinematics without forces |
| Newton's Laws | Net force causes acceleration | Free-body diagram problems |
| Energy | Work and energy transfer | Speeds, heights, compression |
| Momentum | System interactions | Collisions and explosions |
| Waves | Propagating disturbances | Sound, strings, resonance |
| Springs | Force proportional to displacement | Oscillators and elastic storage |
| Pendulums | Gravity-driven angular oscillation | Small-angle SHM and timing |

## Quick Exam Advice
- Write knowns/unknowns first.
- Choose one model: kinematics, forces, energy, momentum, or SHM.
- Keep sign conventions consistent.
- Track whether the system is linear motion, rotational/angular motion, or oscillatory motion.
- Always check dimensions at the end.
